What is Spotlings?

Spotlings is a cozy logic puzzle for iPhone. Each level is a small board split into coloured homes, and your job is to find the one spot where each pet lives. Every puzzle has exactly one answer that you can reach by logic alone, no guessing needed. Win boards to earn points, open capsules and collect new pets, then see how far you get in the Game Center rankings.

How to play

  • Put one pet in every row, every column and every coloured home.
  • Pets never touch, not even corner to corner (diagonally).
  • Tap a tile to mark it with an ✕ (no pet here). Tap an ✕ again to take it away.
  • Double-tap a tile to place a pet there.
  • You have 3 hearts. Placing a pet in the wrong spot costs a heart. Lose all three and the board is over, but you can try again.
  • Hints: stuck? Tap the bulb. A hint shows the next logical step and explains why. You get a free hint every day.

You can also turn on Auto ✕ in Settings: after each correct pet, the tiles it rules out are marked for you.

Questions

I reinstalled the game and lost my progress. Can I get it back?

Your progress (levels, stars and pets) is saved only on your phone. There are no accounts and no cloud save, so we have no copy we could restore. Deleting the app deletes your progress with it.

If you restore your whole iPhone from a backup (iCloud or a computer) that was made before you deleted the game, your progress may come back with it.

Why are there ads, and how do I turn them off?

Ads help keep Spotlings free (from version 1.1). Most are ones you choose: watch a short video to win back a heart, get a hint or a pet reveal, or double a win's points. Now and then a short ad plays between levels, never before level 20, never during a puzzle and never after a capsule.

Buying anything in the shop (No Ads, or any pack) turns off the ads between levels for good. The ads you choose stay on offer, because they give you something; you never have to watch them.

Why does the game ask what year I was born? So children get ads set up the way the law asks for them. It is asked once, and the year stays on your phone. No one gets personalised ads.

How do I restore purchases?

If Spotlings offers purchases, you can open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy. Purchases are handled by Apple, so for billing questions or refunds please use Apple's Report a Problem page.

How do I turn off the daily reminder?

The daily reminder is off unless you turn it on. To turn it off again, open Settings in the game and switch off Daily reminder. You can also turn off notifications for Spotlings in your iPhone's Settings app, under Notifications. The reminder is a single note a day at 7 pm, set up on your phone.

How do Game Center rankings and my avatar work?

Spotlings uses Apple's Game Center for its rankings. You are ranked by the points you win from puzzles (today, this week and all time) and by the highest level you have won. The "today" list starts over every day and "this week" every Monday (both at midnight UTC). Other players see your Game Center nickname, your score and the pet you chose as your avatar, never a photo.

To change your avatar, open the rankings screen and pick a different pet. Other players see your new avatar after your next win (your own row shows it straight away).

The Friends list only works if you allow Spotlings to see your Game Center friends; iOS asks you the first time. You can sign out of Game Center or change friends access in your iPhone's Settings app, under Game Center. The game works fine without Game Center.

Which pets show up on the board?

Boards use the pets you have collected, mixed at random, and trying a level again brings a new mix. Each coloured home has one pet, but it stays a surprise (a question mark) until you find its spot. You start with the one pet you pick, so early boards repeat it; every new pet from a capsule joins your boards.

How do capsules work?

Winning boards earns points. When the points meter is full, you open a capsule with a new pet inside. Common pets come most often, rare pets less often, and super rare pets are the hardest to find. The capsule's colour shows its rarity: pastel for common, purple for rare, gold for super rare.

  • No duplicates: a capsule always holds a pet you don't have yet.
  • Pure chance: there is no trick and no guarantee. Once you own every pet of one rarity, the next capsules come from the rarities you still need.
  • Never sold: capsules and points can only be earned by playing. They are never sold for money.
